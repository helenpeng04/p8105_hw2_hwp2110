The NYC Transit data contains information related to each entrance and
exit for each subway station in NYC, including subway line, station
name, geographic coordinates of station and entrance, routes served,
whether entry is allowed, vending availability, entrance type, staffing,
and ADA compliance. The data were cleaned by selecting the variables
relevant to the analysis, and converting `entry` a character variable
consisting of `YES` and `NO` into a logical variable consisting of
`TRUE` and `FALSE`. The resulting dataset has 19 columns and 1868 rows.
The data is not tidy because there are mulitple variables representing
routes served (e.g. `route1`, `route2`, `route3`). In tidy data, route
would be represented as a single variable, with each row corresponding
to a station-route combination.
